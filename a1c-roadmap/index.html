          Three 30-day phases (Aug 24–Sep 22, Sep 23–Oct 22, Oct 23–Nov 21, 2026) combining Tonal strength training (Mon/Wed/Fri), a Wahoo incline treadmill walk (Tue), and Kickr Bike cardio (Thu/Sat/Sun), alongside a 12-week alcohol step-down, a soup-forward low-glycemic nutrition framework, and an optional supplement stack.
