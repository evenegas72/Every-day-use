            About This Plan

            Three 30-day phases (Aug 24–Sep 22, Sep 23–Oct 22, Oct 23–Nov 21, 2026) combining Tonal strength training (Mon/Wed/Fri), a Wahoo incline treadmill walk (Tue), and Kickr Bike cardio (Thu/Sat/Sun), alongside a 12-week alcohol step-down, a soup-forward low-glycemic nutrition framework, and an optional supplement stack. Your A1C reflects average blood glucose over the past 2–3 months, so this 90-day window maps to one complete A1C measurement cycle — Day 1 is Monday, August 24, 2026, and Day 90 lands on Saturday, November 21, 2026, right before your lab draw and doctor visit.

            Medical notice: this is a personal goal-setting and accountability tool, not medical advice.
