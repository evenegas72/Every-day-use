          🍜 Soup Rotation — Reference

          Soups are one of the best blood-sugar management tools available: high volume, high protein, high fiber, and easy to batch-cook. Batch-cook 2 soups on Sunday — they refrigerate 4–5 days and form the backbone of the week's lunches and dinners.

          What to Tell Your Doctor

          Bring data to the appointment — not just a summary. Screenshots from the Tonal app (workout history, weight progression) and Wahoo app (ride duration, heart rate zones) are legitimate clinical data.

          • "I completed approximately 225–250 minutes of combined aerobic and resistance exercise per week for 12 weeks — both aerobic cycling and machine-based resistance training."
          • "I reduced my nightly alcohol intake from 4–5 beers to [X] per night by week 12."
          • "I followed a low-glycemic meal pattern emphasizing protein, fiber, and vegetable-based soups."
          • "I added berberine (500 mg 3×/day), magnesium glycinate (400 mg), and psyllium husk fiber (5–10g before meals) — here are the exact products and doses."

          About This Plan

          Three 30-day phases (Aug 24–Sep 22, Sep 23–Oct 22, Oct 23–Nov 21, 2026) combining Tonal strength training (Mon/Wed/Fri), a Wahoo incline treadmill walk (Tue), and Kickr Bike cardio (Thu/Sat/Sun), alongside a 12-week alcohol step-down, a soup-forward low-glycemic nutrition framework, and an optional supplement stack. Your A1C reflects average blood glucose over the past 2–3 months, so this 90-day window maps to one complete A1C measurement cycle — Day 1 is Monday, August 24, 2026, and Day 90 lands on Saturday, November 21, 2026, right before your lab draw and doctor visit.

          Medical notice: this is a personal goal-setting and accountability tool, not medical advice. Work with your doctor before starting or changing exercise, nutrition, or supplement routines.

          Meal Timing Strategies

          • Eat within 1–2 hours of waking — breakfast stabilizes morning cortisol-driven glucose elevation
          • No eating within 2 hours of bedtime — nighttime glucose is least efficiently managed
          • Try to ride the Kickr after dinner when scheduling allows — direct glucose utilization
          • Never skip a meal entirely — replace with a protein-rich snack if needed to avoid rebound hunger
